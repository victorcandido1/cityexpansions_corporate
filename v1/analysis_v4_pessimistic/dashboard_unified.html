Key Insights

Worst Case Impact

In worst-case scenarios, car speeds drop to 12-17 km/h — barely faster than walking (5 km/h). Helicopter saves 2-2.5 hours.

Helicopter Advantage

Helicopter is only 20% affected by traffic (car-to-helipad segment), while car travel is 100% affected.

City Differences

NY has more extreme worst-case (5.20x) due to unpredictable events. LA has higher baseline congestion (1.42x normal).
