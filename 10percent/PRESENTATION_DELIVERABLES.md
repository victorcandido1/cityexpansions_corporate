## 🔍 Slide 3 Deep Dive: The Ranking Sequence

### Why This Slide is Critical

This slide answers the most important question stakeholders will ask:

**"How exactly did you determine which cities to prioritize?"**

### The 10-Step Process Shown

```
STEP 1: Data Collection
├─ IRS SOI (AGI data)
├─ Census ACS (HH $200k+)
├─ Census CBP (Corporate data)
├─ FAA (Airports/Heliports)
└─ FlightRadar24 (80.8M flights)
    ↓
STEP 2: Household Wealth Scoring
├─ AGI per return
├─ HH $200k+ count
├─ Population density
└─ → Top 10% identified
    ↓
STEP 3: Corporate Power Scoring
├─ Revenue by industry
├─ Employment metrics
├─ Power industries focus
└─ → Top 10% identified
    ↓
STEP 4: MSA Adjustments
├─ Cost of living multipliers
├─ Market dynamics
└─ → Revenue adjusted by city
    ↓
STEP 5: Intersection Analysis
├─ Top 10% Wealth AND Top 10% Corporate
└─ → 197 "Golden" ZIPs identified
    ↓
STEP 6: Aviation Infrastructure
├─ Distance to airports
├─ Heliport density
├─ Travel time analysis
└─ → Accessibility scores
    ↓
STEP 7: Premium Flights Analysis
├─ Widebody >5hr filter
├─ Cabin data merge (98.9% match)
└─ → 3.1M premium flights, market sizing
    ↓
STEP 8: Cluster Analysis
├─ K-means clustering
├─ ZIP groupings
└─ → 21 service area clusters
    ↓
STEP 9: Temporal Patterns
├─ UTC → Local time conversion
├─ Hourly heatmaps
└─ → Peak hour identification
    ↓
STEP 10: FINAL CITY RANKING
└─ Combined Score = Wealth × Corporate × Aviation × Flight Volume
    
    RESULT:
    1. New York JFK: 136k seats/day, 200+ heliports, $75M potential
    2. Los Angeles: 117k seats/day, 100+ heliports, $64M potential
    3. Chicago: 101k seats/day, strong corporate, $56M potential
```
