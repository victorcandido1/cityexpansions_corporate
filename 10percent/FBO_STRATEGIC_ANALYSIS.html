3. Key Insights & Recommendations

FBO Access Patterns

  • NYC has superior FBO infrastructure: 71% of clusters have FBO at primary airport vs. 25% in LA
  • Multiple operators create competition: Teterboro (4 operators), HPN (3 operators) provide choices
  • LA relies on LAX concentration: 3 operators at LAX but limited suburban FBO presence

Strategic Partnership Opportunities

  1. Heliflite (NYC): Primary partner candidate
    • Proven track record (70K+ hours, 100% safety)
    • Serves 10 of 14 NYC clusters within 40km
    • Existing corporate travel expertise
  2. Maverick (LA): Scale advantage
    • Largest S-76 fleet on West Coast
    • Covers all 8 LA clusters (avg 57km distance)
    • Existing charter/corporate event business
  3. FlexJet (Both markets): UHNW integration
    • Access to fractional jet client base
    • Premium positioning aligns with REVO brand
    • FBO presence at key airports (TEB, HPN, VNY)

Gap Analysis

Clusters WITHOUT FBO Access (12 clusters):
  • NYC: 4 clusters using LGA, N07, NJ72, DXR (no FBO)
  • LA: 6 clusters using EMT, CA42, SNA, CMA (no FBO)

Opportunity: These clusters represent untapped premium market. REVO partnerships could provide FBO-equivalent experiences (meet & greet, private lounges) at non-FBO airports.
